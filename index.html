Connecting

  1. Switch on the traffic light and the car.
  2. Turn on Bluetooth on the phone.
  3. Tap Connect and pick TrafficLight.
Works in Chrome on Android. On an iPhone, open the app in the free Bluefy browser.

AI

Inside Claude, the AI uses your Claude account. Anywhere else, add a free Google Gemini key.

Get one free at aistudio.google.com → Get API key. It stays on this phone.

Install on the phone

  1. Open the app's web address in Chrome on the phone.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  3. Open it from the new icon: it runs full screen, like any other app.
